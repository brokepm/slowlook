Slow Look

A great painting, every day.

Slow Look shows you one great painting each day, from the public collections of the world's great museums. Pinch to look closer, tap to read the story behind it, browse past days and save your favourites.

Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email us at info@coldstartharbor.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Slow Look works without an account or sign-in.

Where do the paintings come from?
All paintings are public-domain works from museum open-access collections, including The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago and the National Gallery of Art. Each painting is credited in the app.

Where are my favourites stored?
Only on your device. If you delete the app, your favourites are deleted with it.

The painting won't load.
Check your internet connection and reopen the app. If it still doesn't load, email us.
